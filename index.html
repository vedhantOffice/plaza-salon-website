Our Story

A Pathankot name built over three decades

Since 1989, Plaza's Salon has been a trusted address for ladies' hair and skin care in Pathankot — opposite Arya Mahila Mahavidyalaya, near Kabir Chowk. What started as a neighbourhood salon has grown into a full-service studio for hair, skin and bridal beauty.

Run today by Naresh Bhardwaj and family, the salon carries forward that same hands-on, personal-service tradition — now paired with a hair academy that trains the next generation of stylists, and a growing ambition to take the Plaza's name beyond Pathankot.
